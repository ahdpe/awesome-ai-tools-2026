# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss the catalog entry or evidence rather than the person submitting it. Harassment, discrimination, threats, spam, and repeated promotional pressure are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, deceptive, off-topic, or promotional content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its contributors.

To report a sensitive problem, contact the repository owner privately through their GitHub profile rather than posting personal information in a public issue.
